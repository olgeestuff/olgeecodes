# Music

TikTok Commercial Music Library tracks can't be downloaded by script. Two options:

1. **Recommended:** export with no music (`music_file: ""` in `editor/config.yaml`) and add the trending CML sound in the TikTok app when you post. TikTok then credits the sound, which also helps reach.
2. For LinkedIn/YouTube, drop a royalty-free track here (e.g. `bed.mp3`) and set `music_file: references/music/bed.mp3`. It is ducked under your voice automatically.
