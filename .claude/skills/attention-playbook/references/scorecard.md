# Scorecard: grade before filming and before posting

Score each 0–2 (0 = missing, 1 = weak, 2 = strong). **Film or post at 16+ out of 20.** Below 12, rewrite.

| # | Check | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **One idea**, stated in one sentence | Sam |
| 2 | **Hook** in 1–3 s: creates an emotion and leaves an open question, spoken and on screen | Sam, Adley |
| 3 | **Payoff** matches the hook, delivered within 20 s | Sam |
| 4 | **Entertainment packaging**: story, character, tension or humour, not a lecture | Adley |
| 5 | Tied to **health, wealth or relationships** | Brunson |
| 6 | Pitched at the right **awareness level** for a cold feed | Schwartz |
| 7 | **Proof over adjectives**: a number, picture or specific | Ogilvy |
| 8 | Uses a **law**: mirror, enemy, polarity, gap, transformation… | 11 Laws |
| 9 | **Retention design**: interrupts every 3–5 s, a loop, no dead air, shortest length | Sam |
| 10 | **Findable and shareable**: search phrase in speech, text and caption; specific hashtags; a reason to save or share | Sam |

When you report a score, show the total and name the **one** fix that would add the most points.
