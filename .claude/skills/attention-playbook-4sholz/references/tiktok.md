# TikTok (primary platform): Sam's Retention & Search Playbook + Sholz's notes

**Core belief:** don't game the algorithm. It's a distribution system that reads human behaviour. Earn watch time and deep engagement, and distribution follows.

## How TikTok decides
- **It's a prediction engine.** It shows the video to a small test audience and bets on whether the next person keeps watching.
- **It's behaviour-driven, not follower-driven.** Every upload is a first impression with strangers.
- **Signal strength, strongest first:**
  1. completion
  2. rewatch / loop
  3. saves
  4. shares
  5. comments
  6. likes
- **Watch time is the currency.** A finished 30 s video beats an abandoned 3-minute one.
- **Specificity wins.** One clear topic tells TikTok who to show it to.

## Hook (first 1–3 seconds)
- The hook is a promise and the video is the receipt. Pay it off within 20 s.
- **Menu:** bold specific claim · the question they're already asking · result first · their problem in their words · pattern interrupt · contrarian correction ("everyone tells you X, that's why…").
- No throat-clearing: no "hey guys", no logo intro. The first frame *is* the hook.
- **Put the hook in the on-screen text too.** Many viewers start muted.

## TikTok SEO
- TikTok is a search engine with a video player. Search views are the highest-intent views.
- Put the target phrase in all 4 places: **said out loud** (speech is transcribed and indexed), **on-screen text** (early), **caption** (first words) and **hashtags**.
- Use **3–5 specific hashtags**, never 20 generic ones.
- Answer **one search query** per video. Title it like a search result.
- Add a **location tag** when local reach matters.

## Creator Search Insights (CSI): TikTok only
- In TikTok, open **Creator Search Insights** (search bar or Creator tools) before planning a batch.
- Use it to see **what the audience is searching for** and **how TikTok categorises the account**.
- **The Content Gaps tab** shows searches with demand but few good videos. These are the highest-leverage topics. **Prioritise them.**
- Claude can't open CSI (it's inside the logged-in app). **Ask Sholz to screenshot or paste CSI and Content Gaps results** at the start of each planning session, then map them to pillars, the STD grid and series.

## Retention and structure
- Make it the shortest length that delivers the payoff.
- Add a **pattern interrupt every 3–5 s**: a cut, zoom, angle change, text, prop or sound shift.
- **Open a loop early, close it at the end.** End on a line that makes the first frame make sense on a rewatch.
- Real value comes before the final third. The CTA goes last. Give first, ask last.
- Kill dead air: silences, "um", slow intros.

## What travels
- Specific beats clever.
- Show the working.
- Make it rewatchable rather than impressive.
- Use trends as a container for an insight, not as a substitute for one.
- Be the source, not the aggregator.

## Pre-publish checklist
- [ ] Clear niche and pillar (health / wealth / relationships), and clear job (entertain / educate / sell)?
- [ ] Uses a recurring format or signature cue?
- [ ] Hook lands in 1–3 s, both spoken and on screen? Does it create an emotion and an open question?
- [ ] Topic clear early? Payoff by about 10–12 s, with no dead time?
- [ ] Target phrase said aloud, on screen and in the caption?
- [ ] 3–5 specific hashtags? Location tag if needed?
- [ ] Native and human, not an ad?
- [ ] A clear reason to comment, share, save or rewatch?
- [ ] Builds the world (series, character, phrase), not a one-off shuffle post?

## Post-mortem loop (after every video)
1. Log the retention curve and name the **drop-off second**.
2. Classify the video: won or lost the hook · held or died in the middle · stuck or lost the landing.
3. Test **one variable** at a time: hook, length or format.
4. Reward **rate over reach**: 12% completion on 900 views teaches more than 3% on 90,000.
5. Turn winners into a **series template**.
6. **Three strikes:** if a structure loses retention 3 times, retire it.
7. Never recommend engagement bait, follow-for-follow or bought views.
