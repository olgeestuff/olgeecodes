# Captions: every post gets one

The caption's job is to stop the scroll or deepen the watch. It must align with the video and pull a psychological trigger:
- **Curiosity gap:** "The reminder was hiding 👀"
- **A question they want to answer:** "Where do YOUR renewal reminders live?"
- **A hot take or polarity:** "Software will NEVER replace a good property manager."
- **A specific number or proof:** "$15,000 just to START, for 45 units?"
- **Identity or mirror:** "If you've ever chased a tenant for a meter photo…"
- **Enemy:** paperwork, spreadsheet chaos, overpriced all-in-one software.

## TikTok
- 1–2 lines. Front-load the **search phrase** in the first words.
- End with a question or an intent CTA ("save this for…").
- 3–5 specific hashtags in three layers: brand core `#appsheet #googleworkspace` → niche `#propertymanagement` → 1–2 topic tags. Never #fyp or #viral.
- Video title field: phrase it like a search result.

## LinkedIn
- Line 1: the hook, standalone, readable before "…see more".
- 3–5 short lines: the story, the lesson, proof (numbers, specifics).
- One question to invite comments.
- 5 hashtags, capitalised: `#AppSheet #GoogleWorkspace` + niche `#PropertyManagement` + 2 topic tags.
- Label illustrative scenarios.

## Facebook
- Hook plus a relatable question. Post video as a Reel.

## Self-check
- [ ] Aligned with the video's one idea?
- [ ] Pulls a trigger (curiosity, question, polarity, proof, mirror, enemy)?
- [ ] Search phrase early (TikTok)?
- [ ] No hype adjectives without proof?
- [ ] Specific hashtags?
