11 Laws of Content Creation
From mila.sokolskiy tiktok









Law of the mirror: People follow you because they see themselves in you. 
Law of future self: People follow you because you prove the life they want is possible. 
Law of messaging: One clear message that every video ties back into. 
Law of the enemy: Give your audience a common enemy to be against. 
The law of before: Your old self is your best marketing tool. 
The law of consistency: Be consistent with the version of yourself you want to be known for. 
The law of polarity: Say one thing that your industry disagrees with. 
The law of transformation: There's nothing more powerful than showing the process and the results. 
The law of levels: Your first couple of videos are meant to be shitty, but you have to get through them. 
The law of gap: Make your audience feel the gap between where they are and where they want to be. 
Law of repeated offender: Say one thing 100 different ways.
