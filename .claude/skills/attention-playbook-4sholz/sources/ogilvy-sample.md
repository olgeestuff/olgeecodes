From Olgivy
“ Stop calling your product premium. Nobody believes that word anymore. Premium, luxury, high quality, best in class. These words don't sell. They just hide the fact that you've got no real proof. Look at the difference. A normal ad would say, "This car is silent." 
A great ad said, "At 60 miles an hour, the loudest sound in this car is the clock." 


That's the difference. That one line sold more cars than a thousand adjectives ever could because it made you feel the silence.


 That's the game.
 Don't tell them it's fast. Tell them it saves two hours. 
Don't tell them it's trusted. Tell them who trusted it.
 Don't tell them it's durable. Show them what it survived.
 People don't believe bigger words. They believe sharper proof. 


Big words get ignored. Proof gets remembered.
